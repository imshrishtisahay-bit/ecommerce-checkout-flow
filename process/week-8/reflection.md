# Week 8 Reflection

## What I Shipped

During Week 8, I worked on completing and refining my e-commerce case study by improving the UI structure, documenting the design process, and preparing the project for portfolio presentation. I shipped key sections including the high-fidelity screens, prototype updates, design system documentation, and design process improvements. I also organized important UX concepts such as design decisions, usability findings, constraints, and iterations to make the case study easier to understand.

I focused on creating a consistent experience by applying design tokens, typography rules, spacing values, color systems, and reusable components. I also refined the checkout experience by considering usability issues such as coupon errors, payment failures, and guest checkout visibility.

## What I Would Change

If I revisit this project, I would improve the research phase by collecting more user insights before designing solutions. I would conduct more usability tests with a larger group of users to identify additional friction points. I would also spend more time exploring different checkout patterns and comparing them with existing e-commerce experiences.

I would improve the prototype by adding more realistic interactions, including better error states, loading states, and micro-interactions. I would also make the documentation process more detailed by explaining the reasoning behind every design decision.

## What I Learned About Using AI to Build UI

Using AI helped me speed up my workflow, especially for generating code structures, improving documentation, debugging issues, and exploring design ideas. AI became a useful assistant for transforming design concepts into working HTML, CSS, and structured case study content.

However, I learned that AI-generated solutions still require human design thinking. AI can suggest layouts, components, and code, but understanding user needs, accessibility, usability, and visual consistency requires a designer’s judgment.

This process taught me that AI works best as a design partner rather than a replacement. By combining my UX knowledge with AI assistance, I was able to iterate faster, solve technical problems, and focus more on creating meaningful user experiences.
